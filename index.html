Nostr Gang #40HPW Challenge

Share boosts for 40 hours of podcasts in a week.

How #40HPW works

A boost counts as a listen
Boost an episode and the board assumes you heard all of it, then adds up the episode's length. It is an assumption, not a measurement, and it is the whole premise.
One listen per episode per week
Boosting the same episode five times is one listen. Deduplicating removes about 9% of qualifying boosts, and one episode has been boosted fifteen times by the same person in a week.
Weeks run Monday to Monday, Pacific time
This Week resets at midnight Pacific on Monday, adjusted for daylight saving. Pacific is the last US time zone to reach Monday, so the board never resets while part of the country is still on Sunday; a UTC reset landed on Sunday evening across the Americas. High Scores is one row per person per week, so the same name can hold several places.
Some boosts cannot count
About 12.5% contribute nothing. Roughly 7.6% are sent to a show rather than to an episode; another 4.5% name an episode this index cannot resolve; and 0.4% reach an episode that publishes no length, mostly live streams. Lengths are filled in as they are found, so a past week's total can rise after the fact.
Accounts that post for other people are left out
A few keys publish boosts on behalf of many donors. Ranking one of those would add up dozens of people’s weeks into a single row. They are named under below.
